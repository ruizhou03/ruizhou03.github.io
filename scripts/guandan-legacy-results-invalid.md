# Retired Guandan strength results

All Elo, win-rate, ranking, population, and win-matrix JSON files beside this
notice were produced by the old independent simulator. They are invalid as
product evidence because that simulator drifted from the authoritative rules
and its rollout could inspect hidden opponent cards.

The files remain only to preserve development history. Do not cite, regenerate,
or use them to name product difficulty tiers. Current strength claims must come
from the server-authoritative simulator, immutable strategy IDs, fixed seeds,
paired seat swaps, and reported 95% confidence intervals.
